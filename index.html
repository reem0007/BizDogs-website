Who are the BizDogs?

Thrive Together

We elect to live together in Lake Superior Hall and take classes together. This creates an environment where members can thrive socially and academically.

The Mission

BizDogs is one of the premier Living and Learning Communities (LLC) at UMD. Our focus is on professional development, networking, and academic success.

Highlights include:

  • Guest Industry Instructors
  • Corporate Site Visits
  • LSBE Faculty Lunches
  • Guaranteed seats in required classes

Why Join the BizDogs?

Higher GPAs: Research shows learning community students succeed academically.
Early Move-In: Move into your residence hall before everyone else.
Faculty Access: Interact with LSBE faculty who help you navigate college.
Alumni Networking: Gain insight from LSBE alumni on career paths.
Study Groups: Attend study groups right on your floor.
Guaranteed Seats: Get priority registration for required BizDogs classes.
